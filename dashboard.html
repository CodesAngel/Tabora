    Workspace tools

    Overview

    Quick actions, and the tools that keep your links healthy, private and easy to share.

    Tools

    Recently opened

    The last 20 links you opened from Tabora, newest first.

    Link health

    Check every saved link, or tidy the page you're on into topic boards.

    Moods

    A mood remembers the page you're on with its theme, wallpaper, blur and private-opening settings, so you can switch back in one click.

    Vaults

    Locking a page encrypts it with your password and hides it, with its boards, until you unlock it here.

    If you forget the password, the page can't be recovered. Tabora never stores it.

    Locked pages

    Rooms

    Share a page with someone, or open one they shared with you. Packages are encrypted and expire after 24 hours.

    Share this page

    Creates an encrypted copy of the page you're on.

    Send the package and the password through different channels. Anyone with both can open the page.

    Join a room

    Paste the package and password you received. The page is added as a new page.
